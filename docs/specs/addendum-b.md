Legacy Plan

What's included: a revocable living trust designed for your family; pour-over wills; financial and healthcare powers of attorney and directives; preparation and recording of the deed to your home; a dedicated session on how your marital property is treated, including heba; and a printed binder of your documents.

How it works: We begin with a consultation. We then design the plan with you — your heirs, your assets, and how marital property should be treated — draft your documents, review them with you, coordinate signing, and give you written guidance on funding your trust.

How is the Legacy Plan different from the Guided Trust? The Guided Trust is completed online through a guided interview and suits married couples with straightforward estates. The Legacy Plan is designed with your attorney and suits families with more to plan for, such as retirement accounts, more than one property, a blended family, or a business.

## **/probate-estate-administration**

Probate and Estate Administration

Summary: When a family member dies, we guide the executor or administrator through the court process in Texas or California, and help families carry out Islamic inheritance shares where the law allows.

In Texas, we handle independent administration, probate of a will as a muniment of title, small estate affidavits, and heirship determinations. In California, we handle probate administration.

When a will or the state's rules do not follow Islamic shares, adult heirs can sometimes agree among themselves to divide the estate by those shares. We help families document those agreements.

In a probate matter, we represent the person serving as executor or administrator. Other family members may wish to have their own counsel.

Does every estate need probate? No. Some estates qualify for simpler procedures, and some assets pass outside probate entirely. After reviewing the estate, we explain which applies.

## **/business-succession-planning**

Business Succession Planning

Summary: For business owners, we plan who will own and who will run the business after death or incapacity, and how the business fits within an Islamic estate plan.

We move business interests into your trust; prepare or revise LLC operating agreements and buy-sell terms; set out who manages the business if you die or become incapacitated; and plan how the business is shared among your heirs under Islamic inheritance.

Why does a business need its own plan? Ownership and management are different questions. Your heirs may each inherit a share of the business, but someone still has to run it. A succession plan answers both.

# **B8 — FAQ answers (muslimlegacyplanning.com)**

Approved. Each answer opens with a sentence that stands alone. Mark up as FAQPage.

### **Is the free Islamic will legally binding?**

It can be, if you sign it with the formalities your state requires. In California, that means signing in front of two witnesses who are present at the same time. In Texas, it means signing in front of two witnesses who are at least 14; the instructions also explain the optional Texas self-proving affidavit. No attorney reviews your answers or the document.

### **Which Islamic inheritance method does the free will use?**

The free will calculates shares using the Hanafi method and says so in the will. Some schools of Islamic law calculate certain family situations differently — for example, when a grandfather and siblings both survive. If you follow a different school, consider a consultation.

### **What if I cannot afford the fee?**

Start with the free Islamic will. It costs nothing, takes about ten minutes, and you complete it on your own.

### **Do you serve families outside Texas and California?**

No. We are licensed in Texas and California, and our services and free will are available only for those states.
